# Ethical Design — Exam Simulation

Weekly self-test drill (rapid-recall style). Say **"exam sim ED"**; I ask ~10 short questions from the
current week **+** every item still 🔴/🟡 in [`corrections.md`](corrections.md); you answer in one message;
I score **content /5** + **keyword coverage /n**, with a model answer. Process: root `CLAUDE.md` §Exam simulation.

### Scoring key

| Score | Means |
|---|---|
| 5 | Correct, complete, justified — **plus a critical/reflective point** |
| 4 | Correct and complete |
| 3 | Mostly right; shallow, or one clear gap |
| 2 | Partial; a key element missing or a misconception present |
| 1 | Fragment of the right idea |
| 0 | Not attempted / wrong |

**"Good" = every topic ≥ 4.**
